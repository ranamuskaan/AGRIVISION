## 🚀 About the Project

**AgriVision** is an AI-powered smart farming decision support system designed to help Indian farmers adapt to climate change and improve agricultural productivity.

The system provides **data-driven insights** using weather data, crop analytics, market prices, and government schemes — all through a **simple bilingual (Hindi & English) interface**.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Indian farmers face challenges such as:
- Climate uncertainty
- Lack of real-time weather alerts
- Limited access to crop recommendations
- Unawareness of mandi rates and government schemes

AgriVision solves these problems by offering a **single intelligent platform** for informed decision-making.

---

## 🧠 Solution Overview

AgriVision integrates:
- Climate & weather analysis
- Machine learning-based predictions
- Crop and soil-related insights
- Market and government data

to deliver **personalized farming advisories**.
